The Quickstart deployment is a team of one. Three directions from here.

**Add an agent.** Grow the team: declare a second agent in `config.json` and restart. The guide adds Theo, a researcher Clara can hand work to, and shows the rules that keep multi-agent work legible. → [Add an Agent](/docs/guides/add-an-agent)

**Write a plugin.** Business logic belongs in deterministic code, not in prompts. The guide builds a small plugin end to end: durable storage, an ungated read, a gated write, and settings. → [Write a Plugin](/docs/guides/write-a-plugin)

**Understand the design.** Why activation is deterministic, why approval prompts carry entire payloads, why agents stop instead of retrying. → [Design Overview](/docs/concepts/design-overview)
