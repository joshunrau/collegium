description: Where to go after the first run.
---

The Quickstart deployment is a team of one. You can go three directions from here.

**Add an agent.** Grow the team: declare a second agent in `config.json` and restart. The guide adds Theo, a researcher Clara can hand work to, and shows the rules that keep multi-agent work legible. See [Add an Agent](/docs/guides/add-an-agent).

**Write a plugin.** The guide builds a small plugin end to end: durable storage, an ungated read, a gated write, and settings. See [Write a Plugin](/docs/guides/write-a-plugin).

**Understand the design.** Learn why activation is deterministic, why approval prompts carry entire payloads, and why agents stop instead of retrying. See [Design Overview](/docs/concepts/design-overview).
